Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: panoramic environment painting for a Chinese cultivation RPG.
Primary request: Huisheng Valley, a natural green spiritual woodland enclosed by sweeping stone walls, where living roots and gently luminous tree sap form the ecological focus.

A few translucent resin beads sit in natural splits in the roots; tiny close-hugging green light is internal to the sap, not floating neon decoration.
Composition/framing: a sheltered oblique view from within the root network toward a tall opening of daylight beyond the trees; asymmetrical layered trunks, one clear arching root gesture, large connected shadow masses. The valley remains readable, not an abstract tangle.
Style/medium: refined softly layered Chinese fantasy painting, clear large forms and generous quiet spaces, deep living greens offset by silver stone and soft amber resin. Deliberate elegant brushwork, gently unreal clarity.
Lighting/mood: cool luminous leaf shade with a small warm shaft of daylight; alive, nourishing and still dangerous, not haunted.
Constraints: natural woodland, not a cultivated garden or ruined workshop. No mandatory creatures, faces in trees, elaborate glowing glyphs, huge mushrooms, omnipresent fog, artificial canals, cosmic effects, modern objects, labels, UI, text or watermark.

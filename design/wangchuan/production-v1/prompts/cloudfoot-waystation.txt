Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide painted environment for a Chinese cultivation RPG.
Primary request: Wangchuan outer harbor, a working river-city arrival point. A broad sunlit river meets a tall stepped bank carrying white-walled, blue-grey tiled Chinese buildings and a substantial city gate. View from the shaded outer stone colonnade, across an open quay to the city rising beyond. A few cargo spirit-boats berth against the current, their hulls carrying restrained water-parting light; small dock workers handle sealed material cases. Buildings remain founded on the bank, not floating.
Style/medium: finely resolved luminous Chinese fantasy painting, quiet dreamlike spatial depth, gently unfamiliar daylight, broad coherent masses and softly layered planes. Not a photograph or a cluttered miniature city.
Composition/framing: panoramic landscape; the arch rhythm, bright river and rising city form three clear large shapes. Detail concentrates at the nearby quay, distant roofs merge into calm masses. Preserve space and visible architecture at small UI size.
Constraints: distinct composition, no prior village reference. Ordinary city life touched by cultivation, not a final celestial capital. No foreground portrait, modern objects, readable text, UI, watermark, thick fog, flying mountain clusters or wall-to-wall glowing runes.
Progression: the first city step beyond mundane life: unusually clear pearl-blue daylight, calm clean architectural planes and slightly dreamlike distance. Let functioning harbor activity remain but simplify everyday clutter and grime; luminous refinement, not another rustic market or an abandoned empty city.
